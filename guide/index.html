If These Streets Could Talk: the guidebooks

If These Streets Could Talk is a site-specific augmented reality (AR) experience on the streets of the former Budapest ghetto. Building and testing it taught us a great deal, and we gathered what we learned into two companion volumes. One is about whether and why to make such an experience, the other about how to make one. Each can be read on its own. Both are free to read and share.

Volume I · Ethics

Ethical Guide for Adopting AR for Holocaust Education

By Prof. Victoria Grace Richardson-Walden and Barna Szász

The case for doing this work, and the evidence for what it does: why AR rather than VR or film, how to represent the Holocaust without trivializing it, how to co-create with survivors and the local community, and how to keep a project truthful and sustainable.

For curators, museums, funders, heritage and memory professionals, educators and scholars · 174 pages · PDF, 12.7 MB

Download Volume I (PDF)

Volume II · Craft

A Practical Guide to Holocaust‑themed, Site‑specific AR Narrative Design

By Dániel Daoud and Barna Szász

The hands-on craft of building the experience: how we found the story in the streets, wrote for a visitor who walks and makes choices, designed the narrator and the moments of decision, built the soundscape, and tested on the street until it worked.

For directors, scriptwriters, AR and audio storytellers, experience designers, producers and technical teams · 158 pages · PDF, 18.4 MB
